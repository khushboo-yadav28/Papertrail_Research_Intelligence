Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Papertrail Research is designed to process research documents locally. This installation stores uploaded files and extracted text in a database and folder on the computer running the application. It does not send paper contents to a model provider or analytics service.

Information stored

When you add a file, the application stores its original file, filename, a locally generated identifier and hash, and extracted text. Derived evidence passages and entity lists are calculated from that text when the library is opened. Questions are processed against stored passages and are not retained as a separate history.

Where information goes

The app binds to the local computer by default. The files are stored under the application directory in data/. The operator of the computer is responsible for access to that device, backups, and deletion of application data.

Retention and deletion

Use the Delete control beside a paper to remove its original file and database record. To remove all local data, stop the application and delete its data/ directory.

Limitations

PDF text extraction may not work for image-only scans. Such documents require OCR before upload. This local demo does not provide account management, encryption at rest, or legal advice. If you deploy it for other users, review hosting, security, retention, and applicable privacy requirements before launch.

Contact

For a public deployment, replace this section with the identity and contact information of the organization responsible for the service.
